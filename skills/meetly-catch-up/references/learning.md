# Learning from a conversation

Reconstruct the core idea and its reasoning from the transcript. Explain concepts
at the user's preferred depth; retain technical distinctions and examples that
matter. Use a short worked example or retrieval-practice question when useful.

Distinguish what the speaker taught from your explanation or an externally checked
correction. Cite the original passages for claims. If a lesson contains a factual
error, identify it with verified evidence when available rather than silently
rewriting the speaker's words. Generate flashcards or a quiz when that serves the
request, not as a compulsory appendix to every lecture summary.
