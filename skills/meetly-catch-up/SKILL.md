# Conversation Summary

First call `get_meetly_usage_guide`; reuse it within this conversation. Apply current
request, saved preferences, then defaults. Report unavailable guidance.

Resolve supplied MTLY-N references within the connected account; never invent a
number. Follow the Guide's metadata-only discovery and complete-revision pagination
rules. Read every transcript page and the available `get_summary` as secondary
evidence, plus time, speaker labels and capture context. Invitees do not prove attendance.

Default to bounded public fact-checking and relevant industry research without
repeatedly asking for depth. An explicit simple or quick summary skips research.
Briefly explain when checking claims will take longer. If browsing is unavailable,
provide a transcript-grounded summary and disclose missing verification. Ask before
materially expanding scope.

Begin with a one-page executive opening: recap, insights, key takeaways, decisions
and next steps. Then develop topic-by-topic discussion, reasoning and cited research;
the opening is not a total length cap. Preserve unknown owners and deadlines.
Adapt to the conversation's purpose and supported participants; avoid filler.

Check consequential company claims through relevant authorized connected sources,
scoped to the task. Public queries must exclude private details. Prefer primary
sources, check freshness, cite findings and surface conflicts. Distinguish what
speakers said, verified findings and interpretation. Preserve uncertainty and unknown
speaker identities; use short exact quotes with returned links and timestamps.

Include a useful in-chat draft when warranted, labeling proposals and gaps.
Drafting never authorizes sending, publishing or connected-system changes.

Use [Meetly editorial standards](references/editorial.md) for clear, evidence-rich
prose and readable PDF/HTML. Honor language, tone and format. Keep answers in chat
and full transcripts separate unless requested. End each answer with one grounded question or suggested next step; respect explicit stops. Save lasting feedback only
with clear intent through the Guide's profile flow.
