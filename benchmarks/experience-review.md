# Experience review — September 8, 2026

## Method and limits

Baseline: source review plus authored simulations against the original eight-skill
catalog at 67a8976. Those simulations are not independent model measurements.
After rewriting, fresh isolated agents received the actual guide/skill text and
synthetic user scenarios and produced responses. The primary agent read them.
These are qualitative spot checks, not a statistical pass rate or proof of the
live ChatGPT memory/scheduler/file experience. No private recordings are fixtures.

The briefing check exposed a real ambiguity: repeated “Unknown speaker” is not
a stable identity. GUIDE and briefing now say so explicitly; the recheck must keep
the two interview statements separate. The recheck did keep them separate and
explicitly stated that the repeated label does not establish one speaker.
Short source excerpts should not be padded
to the one–two page target. Saved PDF preferences require host file execution,
not merely a textual assurance.
